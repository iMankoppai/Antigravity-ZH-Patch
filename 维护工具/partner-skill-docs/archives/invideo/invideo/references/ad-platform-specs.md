# Ad platform specs

Where each cut will run, and what the frame has to fit.

| Platform | Placement | Frame | Keep clear | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Meta | Feed | 4:5 or 1:1 | | |
| Meta | Reels, Stories | 9:16, 1080×1920 | Top 14%, bottom 35%, 6% each side | |
| TikTok | In-feed | 9:16, 1080×1920 | The right-side buttons and the caption at the bottom; a longer caption covers more | 5 to 60 s; 21 to 34 s suggested |
| YouTube | Skippable in-stream | 16:9 | | The viewer can skip after 5 s, so the point lands before then |
| YouTube | Bumper | 16:9 | | 6 s, no skip |
| YouTube | Shorts | 9:16 | The buttons on the right and the title at the bottom | |
| Amazon | Sponsored Brands video | 16:9, 1920×1080, no black bars | | 6 to 45 s |
| App Store | App preview | Portrait or landscape, at the size for each device | | 15 to 30 s; the poster frame defaults to 5 s in |
| CTV | Spot | 16:9, 1920×1080 | Text inside the center 90% (title safe) | 15 s or 30 s |

Loudness for each destination: `/invideo-timeline`.

## AI disclosure

Realistic generated people, voices and scenes get each platform's own AI label; political and social-issue ads need disclosure everywhere. When in doubt, label it.
