# Shorts and reels

## Start from a brief

Know where it will post. Gather the original footage at full resolution, never a copy with another platform's watermark.

## What makes it work

Nobody chose to watch this. The feed served it to a stranger whose thumb is already moving, so the first frame is the thumbnail, the title and the opening line at once. Every second has to earn the next. Restraint is the advanced skill: shorts fail from excess, not from holding back.

- One short, one idea. A second good idea is another short.
- As long as the idea keeps earning the next second, not one beat longer.
- Make something a viewer would send to one specific friend.

## Craft

**Hook.** Open on the strongest line, never a greeting, a logo or the run-up. Picture, text and voice each carry part of it; the text never transcribes the voice. Muted, frame one should say something alone. Keep the promise within two or three seconds.

**Selection.** A clip is a new piece, not a trim. Read its first sentence cold: if it needs a name, a callback or who is speaking, move the in-point or pick another moment. Look for a turn: a changed mind, a confession, a surprising number. Lead with the payoff line, then cut back to how they got there. A clip never says what the speaker didn't mean.

**Cuts.** Cut to the speed of comprehension, not faster. A punch-in lands on the word that matters; b-roll shows what is said, never decorates. Cut dead air; keep the pause before an admission. Hard cuts over wipes.

**Captions.** Emphasis, not transcription: a few words at a time, one stressed word per line, in the middle third, off the face. Bouncing word-by-word captions suit punchy advice, never anything quiet.

**Sound.** Clean voice first. Music low enough to hear every consonant, and out at the emotional peak. A whoosh on every cut is hiding weak cuts.

**Frame.** Face in the upper middle. Stack, don't spread: speaker above, b-roll below. Reframe to whoever matters now. Never black bars.

**Ending.** When the payoff lands, it's over: no sign-off. Loop when the last line can run into the first. A call to action goes before the payoff.

## By type

| Video | Focus |
|---|---|
| Long video to shorts | The five moments that stand alone, not fifty merely fine ones. |
| Per platform | One master, a light variant each: hook line, first frame, captions. |
| Faceless | The voice is the face: a narrator with a point of view. Keep the format, vary everything inside it. |
| Sleep and meditation | One slow scene, long holds, no hard cuts or sudden sounds. Each video a distinct place, never the same loop renamed. |
| Thumbnail | One face or object, one idea, readable at phone size. |
