# Birthdays, weddings, travel and slideshows

## Start from a brief

Know who will watch and where. Gather every photo and clip, and hunt for voices: voicemails, vows, toasts. Learn their music, and who must or must not appear.

## What makes it work

The audience already loves the person. Don't introduce them; give them back. A shaky clip of Grandpa's laugh beats any drone shot. Pick for meaning, not image quality.

- Their own voice is the most precious thing you have. Lay it first; cut picture to it.
- Find what recurs: the same chair, cap, dog or doorway across the years. Let it carry time.
- Let a little ache into a happy film and a laugh into a sad one.
- Shorter than the family asks for.

## Craft

**Voice.** Drop the music under a real voice. Let the voice arrive a beat before the face. The voice of someone who has died plays once, near the end, with the family's yes.

**Photos and music.** Sorting, order, pacing and the song: `references/occasion-slideshow.md`.

**Cuts.** Straight cuts; a slow dissolve only when years pass. Never spins or page curls. Change on the musical phrase, not every beat. The strongest cut is between two times: the same gesture at 4 and at 40.

**Look.** Old footage keeps its year, grain and shake. Never smooth a face until it stops looking like them.

**Tension.** Choose the last image and sound first. One peak in the final quarter, the music pulled out for a beat before it. Then land softly: the last photo held, a few seconds of silence. End on a face, never a title card.

## By type

| Video | Focus |
|---|---|
| Birthday | Open on a laugh. Each message cut to its best line; the closest person last. |
| Wedding | Vows, letters and toasts are the spine. Guests' faces are the chorus. End small and quiet. |
| Travel | People over places, front door to front door, by mood not by day. Keep what went wrong. |
| Memorial | The whole life, their humor, nothing the family hasn't seen. End on one photo they chose. |
| Slideshow | The recipe in `references/occasion-slideshow.md`. |
| Animated photo | Small, true motion: a blink, a breath. It must still look like them. |
| Personal message | Their name and one detail only they would know, early. |
| Sports reel | For a coach: best plays first, athlete marked, real speed, no music. For the team: the losses, every player. |
| Trend edit | Hit the trend's timing exactly. The person stays the point. |

## Rules

Never a licensed character voice. No forensic enhancement.
