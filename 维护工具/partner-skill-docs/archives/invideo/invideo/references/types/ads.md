# Performance ads

## Start from a brief

Know the buyer, the one pain or desire this ad presses, and what it tests; name each ad by that. Gather real product photos and footage, reviews in the customer's own words, and past winners.

## What makes it work

A performance ad is a sales argument that has to pass as a post, for a stranger who decides in the first second. Native beats pretty: match the feed it runs in, never a studio look with a logo in frame one. Clear beats clever.

- The hook stirs a feeling, says who it is for, and leads straight into the pitch. A stunt that doesn't connect is bait.
- Variants differ in what you see in the first seconds, not just the words on top.
- One moment of desire. Everything serves it.
- Vary the concept, not the color: fifteen color swaps are one ad.
- One ask, in the speaker's voice: "link's below", not "transform your routine".

## Craft

**Hook.** The first frame is the thumbnail. Open mid-action, on a feeling or a problem, never a logo or a fade. Prefer a visual hook to a sentence; one strong line can carry many openings.

**Look.** Real places, natural light, phone framing: the product on a bathroom counter, never a white void. Borrow a native format and keep its grammar, down to the font. Don't grade or stabilize the authenticity out.

**Voice.** A friend telling you, not a brand presenting. About 130 words a minute. Lock the voice first, then cut to it. Pick the take that sounds unscripted. Jump-cut the ums but keep the breath before an admission.

**Cuts.** Hard cuts and punch-ins, never dissolves. A visual reset every 2 to 4 seconds. Cut to b-roll the moment a claim needs proof, never to keep it moving. Stay on the pain a beat longer than feels comfortable, then pay it off. Never save the best for last.

**Text.** It works muted and gets better with sound. Captions carry the argument at 5 to 10 words a second, inside the safe zone. Weight one word so the eye lands there first.

## By type

| Video | Focus |
|---|---|
| Hook test | Openings that differ in picture and structure, on one body. A word swap is not a hook test. |
| Placements | Reframe shot by shot, never a center crop. Text clear of each platform's UI: `references/ad-platform-specs.md`. |
| Statics | The winning hook cut to three or four words over the product. |
| UGC with an AI actor | Cut it with real product shots. The actor never says "I love this". |
| Iterating a winner | Protect what carries it: the picture, the words or the feeling. Change the rest a lot: new footage, face, format or reason to buy. |
| Localizing | Rewrite the hook for the market, never translate it. Swap currency and settings; re-time cuts to the new language. |

## Rules

The product is real and the work is ordinary marketing. Label AI where the platform requires it.
