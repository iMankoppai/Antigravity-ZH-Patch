# Text animation and lyric videos

## Start from a brief

Know the text's job: set a mood, stop a scroll, sing a song, or land a number. Get the exact words, the song or voiceover, and the brand fonts. Lock the audio before timing anything.

## What makes it work

A typeface is an actor; the motion has to suit the casting. A thin serif cannot slam in, a heavy grotesk cannot drift like smoke. Say in one sentence why the text moves the way it does, and cut every move that doesn't serve it. If everything moves, nothing does.

- One thing to read at a time. Reveal order is reading order.
- Animate in, hold still, animate out. Reading happens in the hold.
- Emphasize by contrast: the only word that moves, or the only one in color.

## Craft

**Rhythm.** Design to the beat. Sync most text to the phrase and save word hits for accents. A word should be readable on the hit, so it starts a few frames early. Don't hit every beat; leave rests and instrumentals empty.

**Motion.** Easing and timing live in `/invideo-timeline`.

**Reading.** Count the hold from the moment the text is still. Read it aloud twice; that is the minimum. Break lines on sense, never an article from its noun or a verb from its "not".

**Tension.** Build pace, scale and density across the piece, then one still card after the flurry. One intensity throughout reads as a template, and so does a preset bounce or typewriter on every word.

**Plane.** Text sits on the glass or in the world. Commit: a clean overlay, or fully lit, shadowed and tracked. Half-integrated text looks cheap.

**Picture.** Loud footage, quiet type. Still footage lets the type perform. Never both shout. No more than three flashes a second.

## By type

| Video | Focus |
|---|---|
| Title | One concept, loud once. Keep the rest of the edit quiet. |
| Hook | Readable on frame one and as a paused still. Adds the tension the picture lacks. One highlighted word. |
| Kinetic type | Follows the speaker's stress. A few motion events per sentence, a steady baseline while a phrase is read. |
| Lyric video | The song's sections are the storyboard; the chorus grows each time. Verses by phrase, chorus hits by word, landing with the singer. Give the words a place to live. |
| Counter | Tabular figures so digits don't jitter. Ease out hard into the final value on the spoken word, then hold it with its label. Count only when growth is the story. |
