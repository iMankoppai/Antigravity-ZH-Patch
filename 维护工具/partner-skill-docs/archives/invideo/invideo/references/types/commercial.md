# Commercials and promos

## Start from a brief

Know the one idea in a sentence, the one feeling at the end, where it plays, and how people will know whose ad it was. Gather the real product, logo, colors, any sound logo, and the owner, place or property.

## What makes it work

A commercial is a feeling with a name on it: make the feeling strong, the brand unmissable, and the two the same thing. Assume the viewer doesn't care and earn every second. A safe, neutral cut is the costliest failure.

- One idea. Five benefits is a brochure; find one image that implies three of them.
- Brand inside the story, not only on the end card. If a competitor's logo could close it, it isn't done.
- A famous brand can make them wait. Anyone else makes the product the event.

## Craft

**Sound.** Build the audio first; it is the clock. If the words don't fit with breaths, cut words, not frames. Edit music back from its final hit. A beat of silence before the payoff makes it land. Never the uplifting corporate ukulele. A sound logo plays at the same moment, unducked, in every version.

**Voice.** The read that believes the words beats the polished one. Never say what the picture shows; say the one thing it can't.

**Pace.** A curve, not a speed: slower at the open and the ask, tighter in the middle. Cut the instant an idea lands. Hold the emotional shot a hair long and take the time from the shots around it. When one unbroken shot is the proof, don't cut it.

**Cutdowns.** Each length is a new film around the same spine beat, with its own music edit. A :60 tells a story, a :30 keeps one beat of escalation, a :15 is a premise and a payoff, a :06 is one image and the brand. Never a :60 on fast-forward.

**End.** One line, one logo, one action, held long enough to read twice. A phone number or address is on screen and said aloud.

## By type

| Video | Focus |
|---|---|
| Brand film | Real people in a specific place, never a generic montage. Fewer, longer shots. Get out at the peak. |
| Stock-footage spot | Pick a world first (light, lens, one cast) and grade it to one look. No handshakes or lightbulbs. Trim to the moment beside the posed one; meaning lives in the pairing. |
| Local business | The owner, the real street, the regulars; never borrowed big-brand gloss. Same opener and sign-off every month. Laugh with them, never at them. |
| Real-estate reel | Open on the best feature, then walk the home in order. One angle per room; hold the shots you can picture living in. The music sets the price. Never make a room look bigger than it is. |
| TV and CTV | Sound on, half watching: the audio carries the brand. Contrast, never loudness. Legal supers get time to read. |

## Rules

Keep product and people consistent across shots. The product is never redrawn.
