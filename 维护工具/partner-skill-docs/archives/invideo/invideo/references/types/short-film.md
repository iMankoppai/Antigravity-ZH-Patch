# Short films and music videos

## Start from a brief

Know the film in one sentence that never mentions how it was made, and the spine it is cut to: the song, voiceover or script.

## What makes it work

The film is found in the edit: generate plenty, keep a few seconds of each, choose like a documentary editor. Pick an idea a camera could not afford, never a cheap stand-in for one: memory, dreams, myth. Write small: one character, one want, one place. Pretty shots with nothing connecting them are a montage, not a film.

- One governing idea: one camera move, one transformation, one promise.
- Keep a shot only if you would keep it from a camera. One wrong shot costs the trust of all the others.

## Craft

**Character.** Lock the look as stills before any story shot: face, silhouette, colors, one signature prop and which hand holds it. Fix drift with a better reference, never more adjectives.

**Faces.** Let the cut do the acting: a still face after the right shot, under the right music, reads as grief. Save close-ups for the two or three beats that need the eyes; elsewhere use backs, hands and objects. Never cut two same-size shots of one face back to back.

**Motion.** Real speed and real weight; slow motion only when chosen. Vary it: locked off, handheld, a fast push. Stillness is often the most cinematic shot. Cut on motion.

**Music.** Lay the track first; it sets each shot's length. Cut on phrases and accents, never every beat, and trust the eye over the waveform. Drop the energy for a beat before the biggest moment.

**Sound.** Sound gives pictures weight: room tone, footsteps, cloth, the object landing. Rough it up; too-clean audio reads fake. A real, steady voice holds a character together.

**Finish.** One grade and grain across every clip, so it looks like one camera. A strong look hides seams a neutral one shows.

## By type

| Video | Focus |
|---|---|
| Short film | Board in stills, cut an animatic to scratch audio, lock it, then generate what the cut needs. |
| Missing shot | Match the grade, lens and motion around it; it should vanish into the cut. |
| Music video | The song is the script. One device all song, a new idea each chorus, the best image in the last chorus. |
| Storyboards and previs | Answer where the camera is and where the eye goes. Rough unless the look is being sold. |
| Sizzle reel | One promise, tone in ten seconds, only the strongest shots. |
| Book trailer | Sell the mood and one question, not the plot. Keep the hero's face partly unseen. End on the cover. |
| Game trailer | Genre and the core play first, variety after halfway. Never a moment the game does not have. |

## Rules

Label generated shots as concept in a pitch.
