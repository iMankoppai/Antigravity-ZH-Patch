# Video invitations

## Start from a brief

Know the event, the hosts, the date, the place, the one thing guests should do, and who is on the list. Gather their photos, clips, names exactly as they want them, and any printed invitation to match. A big pile of photos, or clips from many people: `references/occasion-slideshow.md`.

## What makes it work

An invitation is a piece of logistics that has to earn its own attention. It arrives in a chat, on a phone, often muted, and is watched once. Beauty that loses the date has failed. The tone is a promise about the event: a guest who watched only this should know what to wear and what kind of party it is.

- One idea true to these people: the dog, the city they met in, the running joke. If another couple could be swapped in, it isn't done.
- A save the date withholds: names, date, city, "invitation to follow". An invitation delivers: venue, time, RSVP.
- It has to be safe to send to the grandmother and the boss.

## Craft

**Open.** The first frame is the envelope. Open on faces or on a title that says what this is, never on black, a fade up, a monogram or a wide shot where they are specks.

**Structure.** A short trailer: a hook, a few specific memories, a turn, then the date as the peak. Spend the best shot and the music's lift on the date, not on a moment in the middle. Save the date 15 to 35 seconds; invitation 30 to 60.

**Type.** Hold every fact long enough to read twice; the date card is the longest hold. Date biggest, names second, place third. Write the month as a word. Script for names only, a plain face for numbers. One reveal for the card, not a separate animation per word. Keep it all in the center, clear of the app's buttons.

**End card.** Design it as a screenshot: names, date, place and one action, held still with nothing moving across it. Then a beat of warmth, a laugh or a look, never a cut to black.

**Music.** A song that means something to them, never the first-dance song. Instrumental under any voice. Land the date on a downbeat or the swell, not mid-phrase.

**Sound.** Build it to work on mute, then let sound add feeling. Anything said aloud is also on screen. A few words from the couple, or a grandparent's blessing, beats any track.

## By type

| Video | Focus |
|---|---|
| Save the date | Mostly mood, one fact. The date is the climax. |
| Wedding invitation | The facts easy to find again on a second watch. |
| Many events | One card layout per event, same positions. A color for each. |
| Birthday | The guest of honor is the hook. Under 30 seconds. |
| Baby shower, gender reveal | Hold the reveal a beat before any text. |

## Rules

Links are not clickable in a video; use a QR code, large and still, with a short URL beside it. Never a licensed character. Names, titles and the order of hosts come from the family, spelled exactly.
