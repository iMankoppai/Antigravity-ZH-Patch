# Product videos for stores

## Start from a brief

Know where it will play and what a buyer doubts about the product. Gather every real photo and clip, the listing copy and the reviews; the complaints in reviews are the shot list.

## What makes it work

The video stands in for the shopper's hands. Every shot answers a question touching would: how big, how it moves, feels and sounds, what comes in the box. A shot that answers nothing is decoration, however pretty. Proof beats claims: waterproof is water poured on it, not the word.

- Open on the product already doing something, never a logo, a fade from black or a mood shot. If it froze on frame one, it should still sell.
- Show scale early: a hand, a body, a room.
- One video, one job. Never a feature tour at one even pace.
- It makes complete sense muted. Sound is the reward.

## Craft

**Truth.** One product reference image in every shot. Approve the still, then animate it. Motion may add atmosphere, never capability. Check every generated frame against the real photo: shape, color, logo, text, number of buttons. If anything drifts, cut the shot. Grade for the color that arrives in the box, then for mood.

**Cuts.** Cut when the viewer has understood the shot, not on the beat. Play the key proof once at real speed in one unbroken take; a cut mid-fold looks like a trick. Speed through setup, never the payoff. Hold the payoff a beat, or replay it.

**Stills.** Order photos like footage: scale, hero, details, in use, hero again. Every move has a destination: a push-in ends on the detail the text names and settles before the cut. Keep one camera grammar, never random zooms. Match cuts on shape and color beat transitions.

**Sound.** Product sound is product information: the click of a lid, the zip, the pour. Mix it above the music. One track throughout, chosen for how it lets you cut.

**Type.** Text names the benefit; the picture proves it. One idea per card, a few words, held long enough to read aloud twice. Never over the detail being shown, never in the bottom or right edge of a vertical feed. Specific facts, never "premium" or "best".

**Register.** Bright and clear on a marketplace, on-brand and steady on a brand page, creator-led in the feed. Lo-fi is a choice, still cut tight.

## By type

| Video | Focus |
|---|---|
| Photo to video | The photos come to life; the product never changes. |
| Amazon listing | Scale, use, the doubt from reviews, what's in the box laid flat. End on a clean hero. No price, promo or dates. |
| Shopify page | A short silent loop near the photos, same light and palette, last frame flowing into the first. |
| TikTok Shop | The result or the problem first. A person talking to one friend, one real-time demo, a spoken next step. |
| App Store preview | Real app footage, no hands. Open on the payoff screen, then how it got there. Plain dissolves. Pick the poster frame. |

## Rules

The product is never redrawn. No stills or slideshows for the shops; use image-to-video for photo-only inputs. Nothing on screen the listing copy couldn't claim.
