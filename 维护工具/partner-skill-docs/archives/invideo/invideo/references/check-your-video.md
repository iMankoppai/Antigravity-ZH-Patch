# Check your video

Before you say done, look and listen like the person will.

- **Structure.** Run `invideo edit check` / `check_timeline` and fix everything it finds, not just the errors.
- **Every change.** Capture each moment you changed, and the frames either side of it (`invideo edit capture` / `capture`). A fix that moved something else shows up here.
- **Media.** Probe, filmstrip and waveform anything new before it goes on (`invideo media metadata|video.frames|audio.waveform` / `media` with that `op`): its length, its frame rate, where the sound peaks, where it goes silent.
- **First and last frames.** Look for a flash, a cut-off fade or a stray frame of the next clip.
- **The ear.** Listen on a phone speaker. Every word clear, nothing clipped, no music swallowing a line.
- **The words.** Read every name, number and URL on screen once more, letter by letter.
