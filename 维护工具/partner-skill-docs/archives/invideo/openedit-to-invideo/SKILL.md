# OpenEdit to invideo

Read `/invideo` first.

1. Render the plan to MP4 at the size and frame rate of the final video.
2. Upload it (`/invideo-media`). Look at it.
3. Edit it as footage: cuts, captions, music and voice. Check the video, then export.

The hand-off in full, with the calls and the fixes: https://docs.invideo.io/integrations/openedit (markdown: add `.md`).
